# N609T Result: H018 weighted-blowup slope form

After `v=s*u` and multiplication by `v^8`, the exact weighted profile yields
a nonzero rational exceptional-slope form for each selected level. Its
numerator has degree nine and five rational roots; one genuine Cauchy slope
pole remains after cancellation. This materializes the blow-up candidate but
does not yet establish a strict transform, resolution, global Cartier divisor,
or ECDLP result.
