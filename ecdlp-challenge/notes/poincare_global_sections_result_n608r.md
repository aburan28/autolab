# Result: N608R selects the two global H018 sections in the cover model

Formal inverse graph evaluation, expressed in the N608Q regular source frame,
has the following origin behaviour:

- cover section `1`: regular;
- cover section `x`: regular;
- cover section `y`: a simple pole.

N608I gives a stable rank-nine, degree-two H018 bundle, hence `h^0=2` under
the stated standard elliptic-bundle facts. Therefore its global section space
is represented by

```text
H^0(F) = span{1, x}
```

inside `H^0(Eprime,O(3Oprime))` in this model.

This selects the pencil but does not yet prove a smooth member, factor-base
inverse, relation rank, target descent, charged cost advantage, or an ECDLP
speedup.
