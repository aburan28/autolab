# N610T Result: generic-direction true-pole transition screen

Over `F_(103^6)(r)`, the direction-independent Q-origin solve and the generic
direction point evaluation give a regular weighted true-pole representative,
a unit transition factor, and the exact transition identity through formal
precision 32 at all selected levels.

This replaces sampled nonexceptional directions with one generic-chart screen.
It does not prove an all-orders transition and leaves the `r=0`, `r=1`, and
`r=-1` charts, global Cartier geometry, normalization, relations, and ECDLP
cost claims open.
