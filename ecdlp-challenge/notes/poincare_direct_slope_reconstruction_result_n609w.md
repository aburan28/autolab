# N609W Result: direct H018 exceptional slope reconstruction

Formal sampling of `-det(V_Q)(s*u)^8 lambda(P,Q)` on 30 deterministic slopes,
after clearing the Cauchy direction, reconstructs and held-out replays a
degree-one numerator for each selected level. Each has one simple base-field
root and avoids the Cauchy pole. This supersedes N609T's invalid hand-derived
degree-nine slope form, but still needs direct normal and reciprocal-chart
checks before any strict-transform or global claim.
