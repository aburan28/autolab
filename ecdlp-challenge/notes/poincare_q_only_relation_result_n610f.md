# N610F Result: Q-only H018 target-relation family rejected

All certified complete levels contain a repeated `Q` fibre with distinct `P`
values: `16`, `28`, and `42` such fibres at levels `0`, `1`, and `17`.
The observed maximum P-fibre size is five.  For example, level zero contains
`Q=(73,86)` with two distinct P values `(63,16)` and `(78,66)`.

Thus no correction `G(Q)`, even an arbitrary group-valued one, can make
`P+G(Q)` level-only: applying the same translation to two unequal P values
cannot make them equal.  This rules out the whole Q-only family on the tested
certified levels, while genuinely two-variable or normalization/Jacobian
relations remain open.
