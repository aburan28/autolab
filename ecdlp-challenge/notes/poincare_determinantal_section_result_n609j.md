from the moving basis at `phi(R+iT)` and an x-value column `v_t`.  The bordered
determinant

```text
D_t(P,Q) = det([[V_Q, v_t], [b(P,Q), 0]])
```

regular chart. A deck shift cyclically permutes nine rows, with sign one, and
the direct determinant check is invariant under that lift change. Mutating one
x-value changes the determinant. At `Q=O`, the leading determinant has
valuation zero. For each level `0,1,17`, nine independent finite rational
P-values reconstruct a degree-nine boundary model scalar-proportional to
N609C's section and six held-out P-values replay that model.

The producer passes six gates and its structural replay preserves the six
claim-boundary checks.

### Failure modes

  its all-fibre applicability relies on the prior N608O nonvanishing/rank
  model rather than a fresh exhaustive determinant sweep.
- The origin result is an interpolation plus held-out replay, not exhaustive
  finite-point equality.
- The determinant has not been supplied with global line-bundle transition
  data or proved to extend as a Cartier section at the true pole and
  non-rational loci.
- This establishes no smoothness, normalization, Jacobian, factor base,
