# N609U Result: H018 exceptional divisor passes smoothness screen

For levels `0,1,17`, the weighted-blowup slope numerator has degree nine,
is squarefree, has five simple rational roots, and is coprime to the genuine
Cauchy slope denominator. This screens the exceptional divisor itself; it does
not prove the strict transform is regular off that divisor, a global Cartier
construction, normalization, or an ECDLP improvement.
