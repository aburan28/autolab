# N609V Result: derived weighted slope form fails direct normal test

At all fifteen roots of N609T's derived slope numerator, direct substitution
`v=s*u` into the formal determinant leaves a nonzero normalized constant term.
The N609T slope equation is therefore not a valid strict-transform equation.
This preserves the weighted-pole observation but blocks any blow-up resolution
claim until the Cauchy/support two-parameter asymptotics are derived directly.
