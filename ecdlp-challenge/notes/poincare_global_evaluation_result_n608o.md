# Result: N608O globalizes graph evaluation through a degree-one modification

Let `F` be the normalized H018 divisor-model pushforward and let

```text
h(R) = (phi_11(R), pi(R)).
```

N608N identifies the graph pullback line with `O_Eprime(3Oprime)`. Universal
evaluation therefore gives an intrinsic global morphism

```text
ev_h: F -> pi_*O_Eprime(3Oprime).
```

The exact rank test over `F_103^6` finds rank nine and nonzero determinant on
all 108 nonzero rational base fibres. The independent replay recomputes the
same panel; the `pi` control has rank one on every fibre. Since the determinant
line is `O(O)`, this nonzero global determinant has its unique zero at the
origin. Consequently the cokernel is `k(O)`, and the image is a degree-two
elementary transform isomorphic to `F`.

The unresolved point is the quotient direction at the origin: this result does
not yet prove that the image equals the particular canonical subbundle
`pi_*O(2Oprime)`. It also does not construct H018 surface sections or provide
source recovery, relation rank, target descent, charged cost, or an ECDLP
speedup.
