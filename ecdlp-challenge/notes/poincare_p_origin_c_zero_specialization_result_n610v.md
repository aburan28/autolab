# N610V Result: finite-order exceptional P-origin specialization

After cancelling the P-origin pole, every coefficient from order zero through
eleven extends from `F_(103^6)(c)` to `c=0` at all selected levels. The
specialized P-origin frame is regular with a nonzero leading coefficient, and
the specialized crossing transition agrees coefficientwise with `-u` times
that frame.

This is a finite-order exceptional-chart observation. It does not prove an
all-orders extension, global Cartier geometry, normalization, a relation law,
or an ECDLP speedup.
