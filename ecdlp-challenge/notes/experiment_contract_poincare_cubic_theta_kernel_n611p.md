# Experiment Contract: N611P Cubic Principal-Theta Kernel Coordinates

## Hypothesis

The three abstract maximal isotropic H018 kernel lines from N606R can be
materialized as exact cyclic order-two subgroups of `E[2]^2` over `F_(103^3)`,
with Frobenius cycling them and the H018 mod-two operator killing each
generator.

## Parameters

- `E/F_103: y^2=x^3+x+24`;
- `K=F_(103^3)` where the two-torsion cubic splits;
- a deterministic nonzero two-torsion point `R=(alpha,0)`;
- `g=(R^103,R)`, `h=(R^(103^2),R^103)`, and `g+h`.

## Metrics

- exact order-two and distinctness checks;
- H018 mod-two kernel equation on all four kernel elements;
- Frobenius cycle of the three order-two lines;
- field-of-definition checks for each line;
- coordinate agreement with the N568 Frobenius graph.

## Positive Controls

- `R`, Frobenius(`R`), and Frobenius-squared(`R`) are the three nonzero
  two-torsion points;
- the kernel line generated by `g` has exact order two;
- Frobenius cubed fixes every materialized line.

## Negative Controls

- identity and F3 graph generators do not equal the H018 generator;
- no nonzero quotient line is fixed by base-field Frobenius.

## Success Criterion

All coordinates and kernel/Frobenius checks pass.  This only materializes the
input for a cubic quotient construction; it is not a quotient equation, theta
divisor, scalar section, relation mechanism, or ECDLP improvement.
