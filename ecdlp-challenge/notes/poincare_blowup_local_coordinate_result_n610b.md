# N610B Result: local H018 exceptional slope coordinate

For each tested pencil level, the cleared exceptional numerator has the same
simple rational root `83`.  Dividing its nonzero numerator derivative by the
nonzero Cauchy denominator gives a nonzero slope derivative of the actual
normal form.  Together with the independently recomputed order-eight pencil
differences, this supports an etale local slope coordinate and the candidate
base ideal `(x+O(u^4),u^8*unit)`.

This is a local, toy-model observation.  It neither derives a multiplicity
sequence nor establishes a global Cartier divisor, normalization, Jacobian,
relation law, rank, descent, cost improvement, or ECDLP claim.
