# Result: N608T rejects the graph-only source inverse for relation collection

The graph identity gives an exact inverse for reachable pencil values:

```text
x(R)=t -> (phi(R), pi(R)).
```

However, the graph reaches only 54 values, with exactly two points for each.
For those values, complete open-level fibres contain 82 to 156 points. The
best graph share is `1/41`; across the full measured open surface it is
`1/106`.

Thus the graph inverse is a correct positive control but is too sparse to be
the source-return engine of a surface relation collector. A complete
projective level-curve inverse or a different source generator is required
before relation rank, descent, or cost analysis.
