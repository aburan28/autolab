# N610G Result: H018 formal slope-degree bound through `u^36`

For all three tested levels, the Cauchy-cleared normal form has vanishing odd
coefficients through `u^36`.  Every even coefficient replays on held-out slopes
with degree at most `r+1`; at the largest order the degree-37 fit leaves ten
independent slopes unused.  The constant term continues to have the simple
root `83`.

This is finite-jet structural evidence for a two-variable normal form, not its
derivation.  It does not define a global normalization or a target-bearing
composition law, and it has no factor-base, rank, descent, cost, or ECDLP
claim.
